Allen statistics

Compiled from the Dallas Morning News play-by-play for all 12 games and checked against MaxPreps. Rushing follows NCAA rules: sacks count as rushing losses and kneel-downs count as rushes, so some figures differ from MaxPreps, where each school’s stat crew counts its own way.

Defense

Individual defensive statistics are the credits entered by Allen’s stat crew on MaxPreps, for the 11 games with defensive stats entered; none were entered for the Euless Trinity game. The credits list 14 interceptions; the play-by-play shows 16.

Special teams and scoring

Kicking, punting and returns come from the play-by-play, which does not name Allen’s kickoff specialist. Return and defensive touchdowns include punt, kickoff, free-kick, interception and fumble returns and a blocked punt. All of Allen’s two-point tries failed; two safeties count as team points.

Game pages

Team statistics and scoring plays are from the play-by-play. Possession time is the sum of drive times in the Dallas Morning News drive charts. Defensive leaders are each team’s top tackler plus anyone with an interception, forced fumble, fumble recovery, blocked kick or at least 1.5 sacks; no defensive stats were entered for the Euless Trinity game.

Stadiums

Allen’s home games were at Eagle Stadium. Southlake Carroll (Dragon Stadium), Prosper (Children’s Health Stadium) and Hebron venues are from High School On SI game coverage; Prosper Rock Hill plays its home games at Children’s Health Stadium (FOX 4) and McKinney Boyd at McKinney ISD Stadium (MaxPreps, Dave Campbell’s Texas Football). The Euless Trinity game was at DATCU Stadium, Denton (High School On SI; stadium confirmed by the editor).

Inside the numbers

Possessions and outcomes come from the drive charts; return touchdowns are not counted as offensive possessions. “Other” outcomes include turnovers on downs, ends of halves and missed field goals. Yards per play and big plays count rushes and passes only. Passing efficiency uses the NCAA formula. Points scored by the defense are interception and fumble return touchdowns plus safeties; points allowed by the defense leave out the 7 points from Prosper Rock Hill’s interception return.
