How these are calculated

Success rate

A play is a success if it gains at least 50% of the yards needed on 1st down, 70% on 2nd down, or all of them on 3rd and 4th down. Touchdowns count as successes; turnovers never do. Penalties, kneel-downs, kicks and two-point tries are left out.

Expected points (EP) and EPA

Expected points are fit from this season's play-by-play: for every snap, the next score in the same half (positive if the offense scored it). The fit uses field position, down and distance, with Allen's own strength factored out so the values reflect an average team. On 1st and 10 it gives about +0.4 from your own 25, +2.0 at midfield and +3.5 at the opponent's 10. EPA is EP after a play minus EP before it. It is a model built on one season, so treat it as directional.

Win probability

Based on the score margin plus the offense's expected points, scaled by time remaining (a normal model with a 48-minute spread of 15 points). The play-by-play has a clock reading only at drive starts and ends, so the timing of plays within a drive is estimated. The chart is approximate.

Dropbacks and sacks

Sacks include 20 confirmed by MaxPreps stat credits and 28 inferred (quarterback losses with 5+ yards to go). Receivers are not rated because the play-by-play doesn't name the target on incomplete passes.
